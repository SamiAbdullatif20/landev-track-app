- windowTitle
- activeSeconds            → seconds user spent on this app IN THIS EVENT — SUM for total minutes
- idleSeconds

- Stop/start same day: APPEND usage; do not clear or replace previous app rows.

================================================================================

- eventKind: "INPUT_ACTIVITY"

- metadata.mouseMovePercent  (same, inside metadata JSON)
